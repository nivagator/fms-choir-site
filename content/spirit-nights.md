There are several ways to support Forestwood Choirs!

## Kroger Plus Rewards 

An easy way to support the program is to designate our Forestwood Choir Boosters as your Kroger plus card beneficiary! Simply sign in to your account and navigate to “Community Rewards” to enroll.  To see a screen recording of how to do so, please use the links below:

## Chick-fil-A Spirit Nights

Chick-fil-A has generously agreed to support our program through *spirit nights*! On these nights, a portion of sales or proceeds is donated to our choir if you use the Chick-fil-A mobile app!
